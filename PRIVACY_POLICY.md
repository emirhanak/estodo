# Privacy Policy for estodo

**Effective date:** May 3, 2026
**Last updated:** July 14, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Emirhan Ak ("we", "us", or the "Developer") collects, uses, and protects information when you use the **estodo** mobile application (the "App"). By using the App, you agree to the practices described below.

## 1. Information We Collect

### 1.1 Information you provide
- **Account information**: When you create an account, we collect your email address and an authentication identifier through Firebase Authentication. If you continue without creating an account, Firebase assigns a random anonymous identifier. We do not store your password — authentication is handled by Google's Firebase service.
- **Task content**: Tasks, lists, due dates, reminders, and notes you create in the App.

### 1.2 Information collected automatically
- **Device and usage data** (via Firebase Analytics): app version, operating system version, device model, language, country (approximate), session duration, and aggregated usage events. This data does not identify you personally.
- **Crash diagnostics** (via Firebase Crashlytics): stack traces, device state at the time of a crash, and a unique installation identifier. We use this only to fix bugs.
- **Push notification token** (via Firebase Cloud Messaging): a device-specific token used to deliver reminders and notifications you have enabled.
- **Connectivity state**: whether the device is online, used to enable offline editing.

### 1.3 Information stored locally on your device
- Tasks, lists, settings, and a local cache are stored on your device using Hive and Firestore offline persistence. Task data is synchronized with Firebase when the device is online, including when you use anonymous guest access.

We do **not** collect your contacts, photos, location, microphone input, or browsing history.

## 2. How We Use Information

We use the information above to:
- Authenticate you and sync your tasks across your devices.
- Deliver reminders and notifications you schedule.
- Diagnose crashes and improve App stability.
- Understand aggregate usage to improve features.

We do **not** sell your personal information. We do **not** use your data for advertising.

## 3. Third-Party Services

The App uses the following Google Firebase services, governed by Google's Privacy Policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy):

- **Firebase Authentication** — sign-in
- **Cloud Firestore** — task sync and storage
- **Firebase Analytics** — aggregate usage metrics
- **Firebase Crashlytics** — crash reporting
- **Firebase Cloud Messaging** — push notifications

Data processed by these services is stored on Google's infrastructure and may be transferred to and processed in countries where Google operates, including the United States. Google acts as our data processor.

## 4. Data Retention

- **Account and task data**: retained as long as your account is active. You can permanently delete your account and associated task data from Settings > Delete account. You may also contact us at the email below if you cannot access the App; verified requests are completed within 30 days.
- **Analytics and crash data**: retained according to Firebase defaults (typically up to 14 months for analytics, 90 days for crash reports).

## 5. Your Rights

Depending on where you live (e.g., EU/EEA under GDPR, Türkiye under KVKK, California under CCPA), you may have the right to:
- Access the personal data we hold about you.
- Request correction or deletion of your data.
- Object to or restrict certain processing.
- Withdraw consent for analytics (by disabling analytics in device settings or uninstalling the App).
- Lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us using the details in Section 9.

## 6. Children's Privacy

estodo is not directed to children under 13 (or under 16 in the EU/EEA). We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child has provided us data, contact us and we will delete it.

## 7. Security

We rely on Google Firebase's encryption in transit (TLS) and at rest. Firestore security rules restrict data access to the authenticated owner. While we follow industry best practices, no method of transmission or storage is 100% secure.

## 8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top reflects the latest revision. Material changes will be communicated within the App.
