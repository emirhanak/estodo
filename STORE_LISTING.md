## English (en-US)

### App name (max 30)
estodo: Tasks & Reminders

### Short description (max 80)
Organize tasks, create lists, set reminders. Fast, clean, works offline.

### Full description (max 4000)
estodo is a clean and fast task manager for organizing meetings, daily work, and personal reminders without unnecessary clutter.

✦ Key features

• Quick task creation — Add a task in one tap, give it a title, notes, and a due date.
• Smart lists — Built-in lists like "Today", "Important", and "Planned" surface your priorities instantly.
• Custom lists — Create color-coded lists for work, meetings, shopping, home, school, or anything else.
• Reminders — Never forget what matters. Local notifications work even without internet.
• Priority labels — Low, medium, and high priorities show what to do first at a glance.
• Fast search — Find any task or list instantly.
• Works offline — Add and edit tasks without an internet connection. They sync automatically when you reconnect.
• Cloud sync — Sign in to keep your tasks in sync across phone and tablet.
• Dark mode — Follows your system theme or set it manually.
• Clean design — Ad-free, distraction-free, intuitive.

✦ Why estodo?

Because your task list should make your day easier, not harder. Instead of piling on features, estodo nails the basics: add a task, get reminded, get it done — fast and reliably.

✦ Privacy

Your data belongs to you. We don't show ads. We don't sell your data. Tasks are stored securely on Google Firebase and visible only to you.
