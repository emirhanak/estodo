# App Store screenshots

Use real iPhone screenshots from the submitted build; do not upload mockups or
generated interfaces. Apple accepts one to ten PNG/JPEG screenshots per device
size, and the imagery must accurately represent the app.

Capture these five screens using synthetic sample data only:

1. **Günüm** — three to five tasks with one completed task.
2. **İş listesi** — a color-coded work or meeting list.
3. **Görev ayrıntısı** — due date and reminder controls visible.
4. **Planlandı** — tasks grouped by date.
5. **Karanlık mod** — a populated list in the dark theme.

Before capture, install the TestFlight/release build on an iPhone, use Turkish
sample tasks without personal data, hide notification banners, and verify that
the new purple estodo app icon is visible on the device home screen. Export
lossless PNGs at the device's native screenshot size; no device frame is needed.
